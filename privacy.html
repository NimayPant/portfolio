Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This is a personal portfolio site. It has no accounts, forms, comments, analytics or advertising, and I don't collect or store any personal information about visitors.

What I collect

Nothing. The site sets no cookies and runs no tracking scripts. The only script on the page highlights the current section in the navigation bar and does not send any data anywhere.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts. The site's typefaces are loaded from Google's servers, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Hosting. Whoever hosts this site may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested). I don't have access to or use that data.
  • External links. The LinkedIn and GitHub links take you to other sites with their own privacy policies.

Email

If you email me, I'll see your address and whatever you write, and I'll use it only to reply. I won't share it with anyone.
